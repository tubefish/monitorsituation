Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MONITOR (also referred to as “Monitoring the Situation,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use monitorsituation.xyz and related MONITOR services.

We collect only the information reasonably needed to operate the service, authenticate users, save account preferences, secure the site, and improve reliability.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use MONITOR, we may process:

  • Account information: information used to create and manage an account, such as your name, email address, profile image, account identifier, and authentication status.
  • Google sign-in information: if you choose “Continue with Google,” Google may provide basic profile information needed to authenticate you, subject to the permissions shown during sign-in.
  • Preferences and app data: settings, saved preferences, feature configuration, and other information needed to maintain your MONITOR experience.
  • Global Chat: if you use chat, we store your messages, account identifier, display name, profile image URL, and message times in Convex. Signed-in members can see messages and the associated name and profile image. You can delete your own messages in chat; you can also request deletion through the contact method below.
  • Technical information: device/browser details, IP address, timestamps, diagnostic events, security events, and request logs that may be processed by our hosting, authentication, or infrastructure providers.
  • Local browser data: cookies, local storage, or similar technologies used for sign-in sessions, security, and saved preferences.

3. How Google user data is used

Google account data obtained through Google sign-in is used to authenticate your account, identify you within MONITOR, and provide account-related features. MONITOR does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, or other Google content unless a future feature clearly asks for that permission before you authorize it.

We do not sell Google user data or use Google sign-in data for advertising.

Google sign-in is used for authentication. The permissions presented by Google at sign-in control what information Google shares with MONITOR.

4. How we use information

We may use the information described above to:

  • create, authenticate, and maintain your account;
  • save settings and provide requested features;
  • protect MONITOR from abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security threats;
  • diagnose errors and maintain performance and reliability;
  • communicate about account, security, or service-related matters when necessary; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may share or process information with service providers that help operate MONITOR. These currently include services such as Clerk for authentication, Convex for backend/database functionality, and Vercel for hosting and delivery.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights or safety of MONITOR or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Cookies and local storage

MONITOR and its authentication/infrastructure providers may use cookies and browser storage that are necessary for sign-in, session security, fraud prevention, and saving preferences. Disabling these technologies may prevent account or preference features from working correctly.

7. Data retention

We retain account, chat, and preference information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Technical logs may be retained for shorter periods according to operational needs and provider settings. Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop using MONITOR at any time. Where account-management controls are available, you can use them to update or remove account information. You may also request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us through our official MONITOR account on X at @monitoringmeme.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request, and some information may be retained when required for security, legal, or legitimate operational reasons.

9. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so you should also protect your account credentials and devices.

10. International processing

MONITOR and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those locations may have different data-protection laws.

11. Children

MONITOR is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. External content and links

MONITOR displays or links to information from third-party sources. When you leave MONITOR or interact directly with a third-party service, that third party’s privacy practices apply.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MONITOR changes. We will revise the “Last updated” date when we make material changes and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the service.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact MONITOR through @monitoringmeme on X.
